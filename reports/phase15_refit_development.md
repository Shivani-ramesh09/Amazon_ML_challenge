# Phase 15 development refit; production gate open

The finalized training workflow checks that the selected policy points to the validated model, that the validated model points to the same feature artifact, and that seed and feature order match. It locks the validated best iteration (320), LightGBM settings, training negative policy, train-fitted TF-IDF vectorizer hash, model hash, policy path, config snapshot, and source commit in the final manifest. The scorer is refit on all labeled training S1 entities in the selected universe; no validation split or early stopping is used for this refit. The held-out 0.885423 macro F0.5 belongs to the **pre-refit** model and is retained as such.

On the corrected 1/16 development universe, 138,401 S1 entities contributed 380,738 positive and 1,534,212 sampled negative pairs (1,914,950 total). Full-pair construction took 3.356 s and peaked at 1.572 GB RSS; the Parquet pairs occupy 51.3 MB. Loading and refitting took 15.075 s end to end, including 11.367 s LightGBM fitting with 10 configured threads, and peaked at 1.735 GB RSS. The final text model occupies 2.25 MB. Saved/reloaded predictions agree on a deterministic 128-pair sample. All 41 tests pass, including invalid lineage and frozen-round checks.

The local host has 15 GiB physical RAM with only about 7.6 GiB available during the run; a 2.2M-S1 full-data refit and its upstream feature/retrieval phases are unsafe to certify here. A naive row-count extrapolation suggests roughly 30M selected pairs and ~4.4 GB for one float32 37-feature matrix alone, before Polars copies, LightGBM histogram bins, labels, dictionaries, and operating-system pressure. The AWS 8-vCPU/64-GB full training run, measured end-to-end wall time, peak RSS, complete-universe retrieval recall and validation score remain mandatory Phase 15 acceptance gates. This report and the development artifact do not satisfy them. No test labels or scores were used in training or policy selection.
